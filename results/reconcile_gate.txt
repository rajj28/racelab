Both tables are correct for the configuration each measured. The gate
asked whether a serialization failure could be produced at all, and a
transaction that abandons its work on one cannot violate anything --
its 0/3 is the signature of giving up, not of being safe. Arm B is
retry middleware: it does not give up, and it does not reconsider.
