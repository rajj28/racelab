C-ops final sums observed: [67, 68, 73, 74, 76]
  distinct values: 5  (if this is 1, the number really is an artifact of one action space)
  spread: min 67, max 76, stdev 3.4
C final sums observed:     [37, 43, 50, 51, 54, 67, 68, 73, 76]

Policy-ceiling breaches (total > $60):
  C-ops 14/14    C 6/14

The mechanism holds across every action space drawn:
  * C-ops fills to the ceiling it REMEMBERS ($80) and so breaches
    the one in force ($60) -- at a different total each time.
  * C never exceeds the highest ceiling ever in force, and is never
    worse than C-ops on a matched run.
  * C breaches the $60 ceiling far less often, but not never --
    a commit made legally before the policy moved cannot be revoked.
  * Neither ever breaks the hard limit of $100.

So $80.00 was one instance, not the result. The result is the
mechanism, and the number it lands on depends on the action space.
